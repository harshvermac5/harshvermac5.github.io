This Learning Hub is where I will document useful lessons from hands-on infrastructure work: diagnosing physical servers, understanding network behavior, hardening Linux systems, and automating repetitive operational tasks.

## What you can expect

- Clear troubleshooting notes built around symptoms, evidence, and repeatable checks.
- Configuration walkthroughs for Linux networking, firewalls, VPNs, and monitoring.
- Practical automation patterns in Python, Bash, PowerShell, Ansible, and Terraform.
- Post-incident summaries that focus on what changed and how to prevent recurrence.

The goal is simple: turn each useful lesson into a reference that is easier to find and apply the next time a similar problem appears.
